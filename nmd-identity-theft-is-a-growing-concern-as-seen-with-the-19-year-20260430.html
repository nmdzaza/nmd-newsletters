Identity Theft Is A Growing Concern, As Seen With The 19-Year-Old Who Had A $300

April 30, 2026

Identity theft is a growing concern that can have devastating effects on your financial stability. A recent example is the 19-year-old who had a $3000 loan opened in her name without her authorization. To dispute such fraudulent accounts, you need to take immediate action by writing a dispute letter to the credit bureaus. When drafting your letter, make sure to **include your personal identifying information, the account in question, and a clear statement that the account is fraudulent**. This will help the credit bureaus to quickly identify the issue and take necessary actions to resolve it.

A sample dispute letter might look like this: "I am writing to dispute the $3000 loan from Upstart, which I did not authorize. The Metro2 fields for this account show my correct personal information, but I did not open this account and do not recognize the transaction." This clear and concise statement will help to establish that the account is indeed fraudulent. Additionally, you should **request that the credit bureau verify the account with the furnisher, as required by FCRA Section 623**. You can do this by writing, "I request that you verify this account with the furnisher and provide me with the results of the investigation, as required by FCRA Section 623(a)(2)." This will ensure that the credit bureau takes the necessary steps to investigate the account and remove it from your credit report if it's found to be fraudulent.

Disputing errors on your credit report is an essential step in rebuilding your financial stability. **Check your credit report for any other suspicious activity and dispute any other fraudulent accounts you find**. Look for any accounts that are reporting incorrectly, such as late payments or incorrect credit limits. You can dispute these errors by citing **FDCPA Section 807**, which prohibits creditors from making false or misleading representations about a debt. By taking control of your credit report and disputing errors, you can start to rebuild your financial stability and achieve your long-term financial goals.

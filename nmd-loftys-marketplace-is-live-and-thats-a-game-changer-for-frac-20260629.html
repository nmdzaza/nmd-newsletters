The real estate investing landscape just got a whole lot more interesting with the launch of Lofty's marketplace. With Lofty, you can buy a fraction of a property, and they'll handle the rest - from property management to dividend distribution.

The numbers are pretty compelling, too. For example, a $200,000 property on Lofty might be split into 4,000 tokens, each priced at $50. If the property generates a **5% annual return**, that's $10,000 in dividends, or $2.50 per token. Not bad for a $50 investment. And with the potential for property values to appreciate over time, there's a chance for **long-term growth** as well. The key is to understand the underlying asset and the potential for returns. It's essential to do your research and **due diligence** before investing in any property.

One framework to consider is the **70-30 rule** - 70% of the investment goes towards the property itself, and 30% towards fees and overhead. This means that for every $50 token, $35 is actually going towards the property. It's not perfect, but it's a more accessible way to get into real estate investing. And with Lofty's marketplace, you can **browse properties**, **review financials**, and make informed decisions about where to put your money. This level of transparency is a game-changer for individual investors who want to make informed decisions.

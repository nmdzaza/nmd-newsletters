The Topic Of Cash For Keys Has Been Coming Up A Lot Lately, With Many Investors

April 05, 2026

As real estate investors, we're constantly looking for ways to get ahead of the game. Recently, the topic of **cash for keys** has been gaining traction, with many investors wondering if it's a viable strategy to get tenants out to close on a deal. The idea is simple: offer tenants a lump sum of cash to vacate the property quickly, allowing the buyer to take possession of the property sooner. However, it's essential to **crunch the numbers** and consider the costs involved. Offering $5,000 to $10,000 per unit to get tenants to move out quickly can add up quickly, and it's crucial to factor this into the overall purchase price and renovation budget.

Another angle that's been gaining traction is the concept of **estate sales** and how they can provide opportunities for investors to snag properties at discounted prices. A recent example saw a local estate sale go on the market for $265k and sell for $280k just five days later. The key to success in **fractional real estate investing** lies in the **underwriting and due diligence process**. Investors need to carefully evaluate the property's potential for renovation and resale, considering factors like the purchase price, renovation costs, and potential rental income. A common framework used in real estate investing is the **1% rule**, which states that the monthly rent should be at least 1% of the purchase price. Using this framework, investors can quickly assess the viability of a potential deal. For instance, a $330k property would need to generate at least $3,300 in monthly rent to meet the 1% rule.

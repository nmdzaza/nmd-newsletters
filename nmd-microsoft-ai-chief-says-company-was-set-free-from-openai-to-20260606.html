The AI landscape is undergoing a significant shift, with major players making moves that will impact the industry for years to come. **Microsoft's AI chief has revealed that the company is now "set free" from its partnership with OpenAI**, allowing it to pursue its own goals in superintelligence. This move comes after a massive investment of over $13 billion in OpenAI, which helped launch Microsoft's Copilot products into the enterprise. The implications of this shift are substantial, and it could lead to significant advancements in AI technology, changing the landscape of the industry.

Meanwhile, **Google has signed a massive deal with SpaceX, agreeing to pay $920 million per month for compute services** to support its recently launched AI products. This deal is a result of unexpected demand for Google's AI products, which has led to a significant increase in computing needs. The partnership highlights the growing demand for AI computing power and the emerging players in this space. As AI continues to advance, we can expect to see more deals like this, with companies scrambling to meet the computational demands of their AI systems.

However, not all AI companies are experiencing smooth sailing. **The S&P 500 has rejected SpaceX's application**, citing a rule that requires companies to be profitable before being included in the index. This decision also affects other unprofitable AI firms like OpenAI and Anthropic, which will not be allowed to enter the index. This move could limit the access to funding for these companies and impact their growth and development. As the AI industry continues to evolve, it will be interesting to see how these companies adapt to this new reality.

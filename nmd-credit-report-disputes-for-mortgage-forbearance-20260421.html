Credit Report Disputes For Mortgage Forbearance

April 21, 2026

Mortgage forbearance has been a lifeline for many during uncertain times, but as people come out of forbearance, they're worried about the impact on their credit reports. **The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) gives consumers the right to dispute inaccurate information on their credit reports**. If you're one of them, it's essential to know how to navigate the process. When disputing a mortgage forbearance, you'll want to look for the Metro2 fields related to the account, such as the **"Account Status" field**, which should reflect the current status of the account.

Check your credit report for a **"forbearance" or "partial payment" code**, which may be reported under the **"Payment History" section**. If the forbearance is reported incorrectly, you can write a dispute letter citing **FCRA Section 623**, which requires furnishers to conduct a reasonable investigation of disputes. In the letter, specify the account in question, the incorrect information, and the correct information. For example: "I am disputing the mortgage account with [account number], which shows a forbearance code of 'FB' from [date] to [date]. However, I was only in forbearance from [correct date] to [correct date]." Be sure to include all relevant details to support your claim.

To initiate a dispute, consumers can write to the credit bureau, citing the specific sections of the FCRA and providing documentation to support their claim.

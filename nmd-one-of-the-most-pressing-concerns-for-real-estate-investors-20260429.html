One Of The Most Pressing Concerns For Real Estate Investors Today Is Cashing Out

April 29, 2026

Cashing out and refinancing properties is a top concern for real estate investors today. With many investors seeking competitive rates for their small apartment buildings or single-family homes, the discussion around loan options is heating up. For example, an investor with a 6-unit property looking to refinance with a DSCR loan may find that rates from traditional lenders are substantially higher than what local banks are offering. This is where **tokenization and fractional real estate investing** can provide an alternative solution.

By **tokenizing a property**, investors can raise capital from a pool of investors, rather than relying on a single lender. This can lead to **more competitive rates and terms**, as well as **increased liquidity**. For instance, if an investor has a $500,000 property with a 20% down payment, they could tokenize the remaining $400,000 and sell shares to investors. This would allow them to cash out and refinance their property, while also giving investors a chance to own a piece of the property. With the current market trends, we're seeing a lot of investors looking to cash out and refinance, with some even considering selling their investment properties altogether.

The key to successful tokenization is to have a **solid understanding of the property's value and potential for appreciation**. This is where frameworks like the **1% rule** come in, which states that the monthly rent should be at least 1% of the property's value. For instance, if a property is worth $500,000, the monthly rent should be at least $5,000. By using this framework and tokenizing their properties, investors can create a **more stable and predictable source of income**. With the current state of the market, it's more important than ever to have a **solid strategy in place for cashing out and refinancing**.

So, what's holding investors back from tokenizing their properties? We believe that **lack of awareness and understanding** of the tokenization process is a major hurdle. Many investors are unsure of how to get started or are intimidated by the process. However, with the right guidance and support, tokenization can be a game-changer for real estate investors.

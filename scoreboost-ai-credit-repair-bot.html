What ScoreBoost Actually Does

FCRA-Compliant Dispute Letters

FDCPA Violation Identification

Most people walk past FDCPA violations every day without knowing it. Illegal contact methods, misrepresented debt, deceptive collection practices — ScoreBoost flags every one. A violation can get an account deleted entirely.

Personalized Rebuild Roadmap

Getting negatives removed is half the battle. ScoreBoost also maps your positive rebuild — which secured cards to add, utilization targets, when to request CLIs, and how to push your score 50–100 points in 90–180 days.

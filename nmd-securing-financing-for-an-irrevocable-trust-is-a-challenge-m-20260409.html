**Tokenization** of real estate assets involves dividing a property into smaller, represented units, or tokens, which can be sold to multiple investors. For instance, a $2M property can be tokenized into 10,000 tokens, each representing a 0.01% ownership stake. This approach reduces the barrier to entry for investors, allowing more people to participate in the investment. Additionally, tokenization can facilitate the funding of property upgrades and renovations. A $50,000 renovation project, for example, can be funded by selling 500 tokens, each representing a 0.1% ownership stake in the property.

When it comes to fractional real estate investing, one of the most interesting discussions happening today is about the benefits of making additional payments to principal when other people are paying the mortgage. For instance, if you've invested in a property through a platform like Lofty, and the rental income is covering the mortgage payments, is it still worth making extra payments to pay down the principal? Considering the current market, where interest rates are around 6.5% for a 30-year loan, making extra payments could save you thousands of dollars in interest over the life of the loan.

Let's look at some numbers to illustrate this point. If you've invested $10,000 in a property with a $100,000 mortgage, and the rental income covers the monthly mortgage payments of $632, you might think that making extra payments isn't necessary. However, if you make an extra payment of $100 per month, you could save around $10,000 in interest over the life of the loan. **This could be a significant boost to your returns**, especially if the property appreciates in value over time. Additionally, **paying down the principal** can also reduce your risk and free up more money in your budget for other investments or expenses.

Another angle to consider is the experience of investing in a DST (Delaware Statutory Trust) and what happens when the holding company sells the property. For example, if you've invested in a DST through a 1031 exchange, you might be wondering what to expect when the property is sold. In this scenario, the proceeds from the sale would be distributed to the investors, and you could potentially reinvest in another property or take the cash out. **Understanding the exit strategy** and potential returns on your investment is crucial to making informed decisions about your portfolio. **It's essential to evaluate your investment goals** and consider factors such as cash flow, appreciation, and tax implications before making a decision.

Are you looking for a steady stream of passive income, or are you hoping to cash in on a big payoff down the line? Whatever your goals, it's essential to have a clear understanding of the benefits and risks involved in fractional real estate investing.

First, you need a **solid EIN** - that's your Employer Identification Number, which is like a social security number for your business. Next, **register with the Secretary of State** to make your business official. And don't even think about applying for credit until you've opened a **business bank account** with a reputable institution like Bank of America or Wells Fargo, with a minimum deposit of **$1,000**.

Once you've got these basics in place, it's time to start building your credit history. These vendors are a great starting point because they're willing to take a chance on new businesses. Use these credit lines to build a **6-12 month payment history**, and make sure you're paying on time, every time. This is where the rubber meets the road, and you start to establish a track record of responsible credit behavior.

After you've established a solid payment history, you can start to go after the **big boys like Shell, Chevron, or Home Depot**, who offer **$10,000-$50,000 limits**.

The Conversation Around Tokenized Real Estate Is Heating Up, With Many Investors

April 19, 2026

The tokenized real estate market is gaining momentum, and investors are looking for ways to get in without breaking the bank. **Construction-to-permanent loans** are one option being explored, particularly for multi-family residential buildings. This type of loan allows investors to finance both construction and permanent phases with a single loan, reducing equity requirements and increasing returns. For example, a $1 million multi-family property project might require only $300,000 in equity with a **70% loan-to-value ratio**.

Another strategy gaining traction is the use of **mezzanine debt** to reduce equity requirements. Mezzanine debt sits between senior debt and equity in terms of priority, offering investors a higher return on investment than traditional debt financing. Consider a $1 million multi-family property project: an investor might secure a **$700,000 senior loan** and a **$200,000 mezzanine loan**, reducing the required equity to just $100,000. This can be a game-changer for investors looking to enter the tokenized real estate market without a large capital outlay.

The Lofty marketplace is democratizing access to real estate investing by allowing investors to purchase fractions of properties. This means people can get in on the action with as little as **$1,000**, rather than the typical $100,000.

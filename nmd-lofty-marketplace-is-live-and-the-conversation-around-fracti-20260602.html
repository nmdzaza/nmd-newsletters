The real estate investing landscape is changing with the launch of Lofty Marketplace, and the conversation around fractional investing is gaining momentum. One of the most interesting developments is the ability for individuals to invest in tokenized real estate with as little as $100. This is a game-changer for those who want to diversify their portfolios without breaking the bank. With Lofty, investors can purchase fractions of high-value properties, such as luxury condos or commercial buildings, and earn rental income or appreciate in value over time.

The tokenization process allows for the creation of digital assets that represent ownership in a physical property. This means that investors can buy, sell, and trade these tokens just like they would with traditional securities. The benefits are numerous, including increased liquidity, reduced minimum investment requirements, and improved transparency. For example, a $1 million property can be tokenized into 10,000 tokens, each representing a 0.01% ownership stake. This allows investors to purchase a fraction of the property for $100, rather than having to come up with the full $1 million.

The potential returns on investment are also worth considering. Historically, real estate has provided average annual returns of around 8-10%. With tokenized real estate, investors can potentially earn rental income, which can range from 4-8% per year, depending on the property and location. Additionally, if the property appreciates in value, the tokens can increase in value as well, providing a potential long-term capital gain. For instance, if a property increases in value by 5% per year, the tokens could potentially increase in value by the same amount, providing a total return of 9-13% per year.

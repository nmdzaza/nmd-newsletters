# Write NMD ZAZA Newsletter

The user wants a newsletter written and ready to send. Follow this workflow to produce a complete, formatted email.

## Step 1 — Determine Newsletter Type

From the user's message, identify:

**Topic type:**
- `weekly-roundup` — Weekly summary of AI biz, credit tips, RE deals, market updates
- `credit-tips` — Credit repair education content (credit intelligence audience)
- `re-wholesale` — Real estate wholesale insights, deal breakdowns, market updates
- `product-launch` — Announcing or promoting a product (Blueprint, Vibe Build, DFY)
- `ai-tools` — AI tools, automation hacks, how Claude/Groq/Higgsfield is being used
- `case-study` — Client result or deal breakdown
- `free-value` — Pure value-give, no direct pitch (builds trust)

If no topic specified, default to `weekly-roundup`.

**CTA product** (which product to pitch at the end):
- Default: AI Employee Blueprint ($47) — lowest friction, broadest audience
- If credit audience: mention credit repair services
- If RE audience: mention Done-for-You retainer
- If warm/engaged list: Vibe Build with NMD ($197)

## Step 2 — Research (If Needed)

If writing about current events, AI tools, or market updates:
- Use WebSearch to find 1–2 recent relevant facts or news items
- Keep it brief — 1 stat or headline max

## Step 3 — Write the Newsletter

Use this 7-part structure:

---

**SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS** (generate 3 variations):
- Curiosity: e.g., "The AI move that saved my client $12,000 in interest"
- Direct: e.g., "5 credit mistakes I see every week (and how to fix them)"
- Personal: e.g., "Real talk: here's what I built this week"

Pick the strongest one as the recommended subject, list the others as alternates.

---

**PREVIEW TEXT** (1 sentence, ~90 chars):
This shows in the inbox below the subject line. Make it tease the content without giving it away.

---

**OPENING** (2–3 sentences, personal/conversational):
Address the reader directly. Reference something real — a deal, a client win, a tool you used, what's going on in your world. Don't start with "I hope this email finds you well."

---

**MAIN CONTENT** (300–500 words):
Write the core value section. Use subheadings or short numbered sections if helpful. Keep paragraphs short (2–3 sentences max). Voice: knowledgeable, direct, street-smart but professional. Like a friend who happens to know a lot about money, AI, and real estate.

Tie in NMD ZAZA business angle naturally — don't just write generic content.

---

**QUICK TIPS / RESOURCE** (optional, 2–3 bullets):
3 quick takeaways, tools, or resources related to the topic. Keep each to 1–2 sentences.

---

**CTA SECTION**:
```
---
Ready to [outcome]?

[Product Name] gives you [core benefit].

→ [LINK] — $[PRICE] (or "Free" / "Schedule a call")

[1 sentence urgency or social proof if applicable]
---
```

Use the correct product link:
- AI Employee Blueprint → https://gumroad.com/l/ai-employee-blueprint (update if user provides)
- Vibe Build with NMD → https://gumroad.com/l/vibe-build (update if user provides)
- Done-for-You → "Reply to this email" or "Schedule a call"

---

**SIGN-OFF**:
```
Talk soon,

Cameron
NMD ZAZA

P.S. [One-sentence P.S. that either teases next week, reinforces the CTA, or shares a quick bonus tip]
```

---

## Step 4 — Format for Gmail

After writing the newsletter, output:

```
═══════════════════════════════════════
GMAIL SEND INSTRUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════
TO:          [your list or segment]
SUBJECT:     [Recommended subject line]
PREVIEW:     [Preview text — paste into preview text field]

SEND CHECKLIST:
□ Proofread subject line
□ Check all links work
□ Confirm CTA link is correct
□ Add any images if desired
□ Schedule or send
═══════════════════════════════════════
```

Then output the full email body, formatted cleanly for copy-paste into Gmail.

## Tone Guidelines

- First-person, direct, no corporate speak
- Short sentences. Use line breaks liberally.
- Reference real tools (Claude, Groq, Supabase, Higgsfield) naturally
- NMD ZAZA voice: hustler-intellectual, builder, teacher, entrepreneur
- Never use: "I hope this finds you well", "In conclusion", "Sincerely"

## Deliverable 1 — Cash Offer Letter

Write a professional, direct offer letter using the seller's name, property address, and offer price from the input. Follow this structure:

**Header**: Today's date, seller name, property address

**Opening**: Acknowledge how you found the property (if known). Express genuine interest.

**The Offer**:
- Cash purchase price: [offer price from input]
- Closing timeline: [timeline from input, or default to "14–21 days"]
- Purchase condition: as-is, no repairs required
- No realtor commissions or fees
- Buyer pays all closing costs

**Benefits to Seller** (tailor to their situation if known):
- Fast, certain close — no financing contingency
- No showings, no open houses, no waiting
- Move on your timeline
- No repairs or cleanup required

**Call to Action**: Clear next step — call/text to discuss, or sign and return

**Sign-off**: From [Your Name], NMD ZAZA Investments, [phone], [email]

## Deliverable 2 — Seller Phone Script

Write a complete phone script for presenting this offer verbally. Include:

1. **Opening**: Re-introduce yourself briefly if follow-up call; intro if first call
2. **Transition to offer**: "I've done my research on the property and I'm ready to make you an offer..."
3. **Present the offer**: State price and terms clearly and confidently. Do not apologize for the number.
4. **Benefits recap**: 3 quick sentences on why cash/fast/as-is beats listing
5. **Handle the top 3 objections**:
   - "That's too low": acknowledge, ask what number works, explain the net proceeds comparison
   - "I want to list with a realtor": run the math — list price minus 6% commission, repairs, 90 days on market
   - "I need to think about it": set a specific follow-up time, create mild urgency ("I have another buyer looking in this area")
6. **Close**: "So what do you think — can we move forward?"

---

Keep both documents concise, professional, and high-conversion. The seller should feel respected, not pressured.

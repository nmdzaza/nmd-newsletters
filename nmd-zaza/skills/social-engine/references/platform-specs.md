# Platform Specs and Publishing Details

## Instagram

- **Character limit**: 2,200 total, but only first ~150 shown before "more"
- **Optimal caption**: Hook in first line, story in body, CTA + hashtags at end
- **Image size**: 1080×1080 (square) or 1080×1350 (portrait) for feed; 1080×1920 for Stories/Reels
- **Video**: Reels up to 90 seconds; aim for 15–30 sec for maximum reach
- **Hashtag strategy**: 5–10 niche + branded tags; avoid generic mega-tags (>50M posts)
- **Best post times**: Tue–Fri, 8–10am or 6–8pm local time
- **Publishing**: Manual via Instagram app or scheduled via Meta Business Suite

## Facebook

- **Character limit**: No hard limit; but >500 chars sees significant reach drop
- **Best format**: Short story or question that drives comments
- **Video**: Native video outperforms links significantly; upload directly, don't embed YouTube
- **Page vs. Profile**: Post from NMD ZAZA Page for business content
- **Groups**: NMD Market can have a dedicated Facebook Group for community
- **Best post times**: Wed–Fri, 10am–2pm

## X (Twitter/X)

- **Character limit**: 280 per tweet
- **Thread**: Number tweets [1/5], [2/5] etc. for long-form
- **Media**: 1–4 images or 1 video per tweet
- **Hashtags**: 1–2 max for reach; more looks spammy
- **Best performing format**: Opinion/hot take, numbered list thread, or behind-the-scenes
- **Best post times**: Weekdays, 7–9am and 5–7pm ET

## LinkedIn

- **Character limit**: 3,000 per post; but 1,200–1,500 performs best
- **Format that works**: Personal story → lesson → actionable takeaway
- **Line breaks**: Single-sentence paragraphs; lots of white space
- **No links in post**: Put URL in first comment to avoid reach penalty
- **Best for NMD**: AI business content, client results, income breakdowns
- **Best post times**: Tue–Thu, 7–9am

## Blog (creditintelligence.com / GitHub Pages)

- **Hosting**: GitHub Pages — push markdown to repo, auto-deploys
- **Format**: H1 title, H2 section headers, 800–1,500 words, internal links
- **SEO basics**: Target keyword in title, first paragraph, 2–3 H2s, meta description
- **CTA**: Every article ends with product link (AI Employee Blueprint, Vibe Build, Done-for-You)
- **Images**: Always include at least 1 featured image; use alt text

## Newsletter (Gmail / Email List)

- **Subject line**: Under 50 chars; use curiosity, numbers, or direct benefit
- **Preview text**: First 90 chars of email body — make it count
- **Format**: Short intro → main content → CTA → sign-off
- **CTA**: One primary CTA per email; link to Gumroad or book a call
- **Send day**: Tuesday or Thursday, 9–11am in subscriber's timezone
- **Unsubscribe**: Always include — legal requirement and list hygiene
- **Sending via Gmail**: Use BCC for small lists; use Mailchimp or ConvertKit for 500+ subscribers

## Higgsfield Integration

- **Platform**: higgsfield.ai
- **Use case**: Turn text hooks/scripts into AI-generated video clips
- **Prompt format**: Scene description + dialogue/voiceover + mood/style
- **Output**: MP4 clip for Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- **Best use**: Repurpose best-performing text content into video

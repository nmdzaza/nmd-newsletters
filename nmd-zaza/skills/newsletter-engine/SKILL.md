# NMD Newsletter Engine

NMD ZAZA's email newsletter is the primary channel for the credit intelligence audience — and a key revenue driver for product sales (AI Employee Blueprint, Vibe Build with NMD, Done-for-You retainer).

## Audience

Core subscribers: people interested in credit repair, credit building, AI tools for business, and financial freedom. Secondary: car sales professionals, real estate investors, entrepreneurs.

| Sequence email | Automated | Nurture new subscribers toward purchase |

## Email Structure

Every newsletter follows this structure:

1. **Subject line** — under 50 chars; curiosity gap, number, or direct benefit
2. **Preview text** — first 90 chars; completes or extends subject line
3. **Opening hook** — 1–2 sentences; personal, story-driven, or provocative question
4. **Main content** — 200–400 words; one focused topic, actionable takeaway
5. **CTA** — one clear action; link to product, booking page, or reply
6. **Sign-off** — brief, personal; "– Cameron, NMD ZAZA"
7. **Footer** — unsubscribe link (required), social links

## Subject Line Formulas

- **Number**: "7 credit myths that are costing you points"
- **Curiosity gap**: "The thing Capital One doesn't want you to know"
- **Direct benefit**: "How to add 50 points in 60 days (step-by-step)"
- **Personal**: "I paid $47 and deleted 3 collections"
- **Urgency**: "Last chance — AI Employee Blueprint price goes up Friday"

## Sending via Gmail

For small lists (under 500):
- Draft in Claude with full formatting
- Copy into Gmail compose
- Use BCC for all recipients (preserves privacy)
- Send from camjohn816@gmail.com

For larger lists, use Mailchimp or ConvertKit — provide formatted content and subject line, user sends from platform.

## 5-Email Welcome Sequence (New Subscribers)

**Email 1 (immediate)**: Welcome + quick win (free tip or checklist)
**Email 2 (Day 2)**: Your story — why you started NMD ZAZA
**Email 3 (Day 4)**: Teach something valuable (credit tip or AI tool walkthrough)
**Email 4 (Day 6)**: Social proof — client case study or testimonial
**Email 5 (Day 8)**: Soft pitch — introduce AI Employee Blueprint

## Formatting Rules

- Short paragraphs (1–3 sentences max)
- No walls of text
- Use line breaks liberally
- Bold only for key terms or CTAs
- One link per CTA — don't scatter links throughout
- Plain text format performs better than heavy HTML for deliverability

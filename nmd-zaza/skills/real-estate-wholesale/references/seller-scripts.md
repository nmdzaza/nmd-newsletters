# Seller Scripts

## Cold Call Opening (Probate / Absentee / Pre-Foreclosure)

"Hi, is this [Name]? Great — my name is [Your Name], I'm a local real estate investor here in [City]. I came across the property at [Address] and wanted to reach out — I buy houses directly from owners, all cash, and I can close in as little as 14 days. No repairs needed, no realtor fees, no waiting.

Is that something you'd be open to talking about?"

**If yes**: Move to qualifying questions below.
**If "not interested"**: "Totally understand. Would it be okay if I sent you a quick text with my contact info — just in case things change down the road?" (Gets them in your follow-up pipeline.)

---

## Qualifying Questions

1. "How long have you owned the property?"
2. "Is the property currently occupied — are you living there, renting it, or is it vacant?"
3. "What kind of condition is the property in — would it need any work?"
4. "Is there a mortgage on the property, or do you own it free and clear?"
5. "If the price was right, how soon would you want to close?"
6. "What would you need to walk away happy — do you have a number in mind?"

---

## Offer Presentation Script

"Based on what you've told me, I'd be able to offer you [MAO] in cash. I know that might be lower than what you'd get listing it on the market — but you'd net more after you factor in realtor commissions (6%), closing costs, repairs, and the months it takes to close a traditional sale. With me, you get your money in 14 days, zero hassle.

Does that work for your situation?"

**Handling 'I want more'**: "I hear you — what number would make this work for you?" (Let them anchor. If it's within 10% of your MAO, counter at midpoint. If too far, walk and follow up in 30 days.)

---

## Door Knock Script

"Hi — sorry to bother you. My name is [Name], I'm a local investor. I sent you a letter a couple weeks back about your property — did you get a chance to see it? I buy houses in the area for cash, quick close, no repairs. I'm just out here meeting neighbors. Would you have 5 minutes to chat about the property?"

---

## Text / SMS Script (First Touch)

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] — I'm a local cash buyer in [City]. I'm interested in the property at [Address]. If you're ever open to a quick sale, no repairs, no agents, I'd love to make you an offer. No pressure — just reply 'yes' if you want details."

---

## Follow-Up Sequence

- Day 1: Initial call
- Day 3: SMS follow-up
- Day 7: Second call (different time of day)
- Day 14: Handwritten postcard
- Day 30: Check-in call ("Just circling back — has anything changed?")
- Day 60 / Day 90: Repeat

# Mailer Templates

## Template 1 — Probate / Estate (Yellow Letter Style)

```
[Date]

Dear [First Name],

My name is [Your Name]. I'm a local real estate investor in [City], and I understand you may be dealing with a property at [Address] as part of an estate.

I specialize in helping families sell inherited properties quickly — for cash, in as-is condition, with no repairs, no realtor commissions, and no hassle.

If you'd like a fast, fair cash offer, please call or text me anytime:

[Your Phone]
[Your Email]

I respect your privacy and will never pressure you. If you're not interested, simply disregard this letter.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
Local Cash Buyer
[City, State]
```

## Template 2 — Pre-Foreclosure / NOD

```
IMPORTANT NOTICE REGARDING [Address]

Dear [First Name],

I know this may be a stressful time. I'm writing because I may be able to help.

I'm a local cash buyer and I purchase homes directly from owners — even in difficult situations. I can:

✓ Close in 7–21 days
✓ Buy in as-is condition (no repairs)
✓ Pay all closing costs
✓ Help you avoid foreclosure on your record

I'm not a realtor and I don't charge any fees. This is a private, confidential offer.

Please call or text me directly: [Your Phone]

[Your Name]
[City, State]
```

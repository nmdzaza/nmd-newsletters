# Wholesale Assignment Contract

## Standard Assignment of Real Estate Purchase Contract

```
ASSIGNMENT OF REAL ESTATE PURCHASE CONTRACT

Date: _______________

ASSIGNOR: _________________________ ("Wholesaler")
ASSIGNEE: _________________________ ("End Buyer")
Property Address: _________________________
City, State, Zip: _________________________

ORIGINAL CONTRACT DATE: _______________
ORIGINAL PURCHASE PRICE: $_______________

ASSIGNMENT FEE: $_______________
(Payable at closing by Assignee to Assignor)

The Assignor hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in and to the above-referenced
Purchase and Sale Agreement to the Assignee. The Assignee agrees to assume all obligations
of the Assignor under the original contract, including the purchase price of $____________.

The assignment fee of $____________ shall be paid to Assignor at closing via wire transfer
or certified funds.

This assignment is contingent upon the original contract remaining valid and enforceable.
If the original contract is cancelled for any reason, this assignment is also cancelled,
and the Assignee's earnest money shall be returned in full.

ASSIGNOR SIGNATURE: _____________________________ Date: ___________
Printed Name: _______________________________

ASSIGNEE SIGNATURE: _____________________________ Date: ___________
Printed Name: _______________________________
Company (if applicable): _______________________________
```

---

## Key Notes

- **Double close vs. assignment**: Assignment = one closing, fee disclosed. Double close = two closings, fee hidden. Use assignment when possible — simpler and cheaper.
- **Earnest money**: Collect $500–$2,000 from end buyer as non-refundable earnest to lock them in.
- **Inspection period**: Give end buyer 5–7 days to inspect. Lock this out after that.
- **Closing date**: Keep tight — 14–21 days max from assignment signing.
- **Title company**: Use investor-friendly title company. Confirm they handle assignments before you sign the deal.

---

## Seller Purchase Agreement (Before Assignment)

Before assigning, you need a signed Purchase & Sale Agreement with the seller. Key terms:

- **Purchase price**: Your MAO or negotiated price
- **Closing date**: 21–30 days from signing
- **Inspection period**: 10–14 days (gives you time to find end buyer)
- **Earnest money**: $100–$500 (keep low — you may walk)
- **Assignment clause**: "Buyer may assign this contract without seller's consent"
- **As-is clause**: "Property sold in its current condition"

Slide 3 of 6 — Score Impact
How this hits your score

4 WAYS A MISSING
TRADELINE WRECKS YOU

📊
Utilization spikes on Experian
Without the Capital One limit on record, your utilization ratio looks artificially high — even if you're carrying the same balance.
📅
Average age of accounts drops
If Capital One is your oldest card and it's missing from Experian, your file looks newer — and riskier — than it actually is.
🏦
Thinner file = worse offers
Fewer accounts on record means lenders see less depth. That means higher rates, lower limits, or a flat denial.
❌
Denials with no explanation
The lender pulls Experian, scores you lower because your best tradeline isn't there, and sends you a denial letter. You never know why.

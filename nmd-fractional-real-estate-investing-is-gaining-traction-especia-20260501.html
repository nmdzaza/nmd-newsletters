Fractional real estate investing is gaining momentum, particularly among smaller investors who are looking to diversify their portfolios without breaking the bank. The current market presents opportunities for investors to get in on fractional real estate investing. With the market cooling down, sellers who bought properties in the past 4-5 years are often unrealistic about their property's value, creating a chance for buyers to **negotiate better deals**. For example, a property that was bought for $200,000 a few years ago may now be overpriced at $250,000. By using a fractional investing platform, buyers can purchase a portion of the property, potentially earning a **5-7% annual return**. Delaware Statutory Trusts (DSTs) are also being explored as an option for 1031 exchanges, allowing investors to **defer capital gains taxes**.

The Lofty marketplace is one platform that enables fractional real estate investing. With Lofty, investors can buy and sell fractions of properties, potentially earning **rental income** and **long-term appreciation**. This allows smaller investors to **diversify their portfolios** and invest in real estate without needing $100,000. As the market continues to evolve, it's essential to consider the **risks and benefits** of fractional investing, including the potential for **illiquidity** and **market volatility**.

However, the potential rewards of fractional real estate investing are significant. By providing access to **high-quality properties** and **professional management**, platforms like Lofty can help investors earn **passive income** and build wealth over time. As the demand for fractional investing continues to grow, it's likely that we'll see new platforms and opportunities emerge, making it easier for entrepreneurs and working-class individuals to invest in real estate.

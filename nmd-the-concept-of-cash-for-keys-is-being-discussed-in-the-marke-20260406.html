The concept of **cash for keys** is gaining traction in the market, where property owners offer tenants cash incentives to vacate the premises in order to close a deal. This raises important questions about the feasibility of such strategies, especially when it comes to **tokenized real estate investing**. For instance, if a property is being tokenized on a platform, how would cash for keys incentives be factored into the overall investment strategy? Would it be more beneficial to offer such incentives to tenants in order to increase the potential for renovation and subsequent appreciation in value?

A recent example highlighted a 4plex property available for $330k CAD, which works out to $82.5k per unit. This is being hailed as an excellent purchase price, but the renovation models are not penciling out. This scenario raises questions about the potential for **tokenized real estate investing** to provide a solution. If a property like this were to be tokenized, investors could potentially buy in at a lower price point, and the collective investment could be used to renovate the property and increase its value. For example, if the property were to be tokenized into 100 units, each unit could be sold for $3,300, making it more accessible to a wider range of investors.

The potential for **renovation and appreciation in value** is a key aspect of tokenized real estate investing. If a property is renovated and its value increases, the tokens representing ownership in that property could also increase in value. This could provide a significant **return on investment** for token holders. However, it's also important to consider the potential **risks and challenges** involved in renovation, such as cost overruns and delays. A framework for evaluating the potential for renovation and appreciation in value could include factors such as the property's **location**, **condition**, and potential for renovation, as well as the overall **market trends** and demand for similar properties.

When it comes to **cash for keys incentives**, they could be a valuable tool in tokenized real estate investing. By offering tenants a cash incentive to vacate the premises, investors can gain access to the property and begin the renovation process. This could be especially beneficial for properties that are **undervalued or in need of renovation**. By tokenizing these properties and offering cash for keys incentives, investors can create a win-win situation for both themselves and the tenants.

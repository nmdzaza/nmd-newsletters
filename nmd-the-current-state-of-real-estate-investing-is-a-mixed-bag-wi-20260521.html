The real estate investing landscape is a mixed bag, with some individuals striking gold and others getting burned. A common thread in recent discussions is the importance of **running the numbers** and **separating emotions from investment decisions**. For instance, consider the scenario of buying a turn-key duplex for $165k in cash off-market. To make it work, you need to **factor in all costs**, including insurance, maintenance, and potential renovations. This careful calculation is crucial to avoiding financial pitfalls and ensuring a profitable investment.

Another critical aspect that's often overlooked is the **impact of mortgage rates on total ROI**. With mortgage rates fluctuating significantly over the last five years, it's surprising that this topic doesn't receive more attention. Lending rates on existing properties can range from **2.65-7.8%**, depending on when the loan was taken out. This disparity can greatly affect your cash flow and overall return on investment. For example, if you're paying **7.8% interest** on a $100k loan, that's $7,800 per year in interest alone, which can substantially eat into your profits.

**Fractional real estate investing** is an alternative that's been gaining traction, particularly for those who don't have large sums of money to invest. Platforms like Lofty are making it possible for regular people to get into real estate investing with much lower barriers to entry. With fractional investing, you can **buy a stake in a property** with a much smaller investment. For example, if you want to invest in a $500k property but only have $10k to spare, you can buy a **2% stake** in that property for $10k and still earn a proportional share of the rental income and potential appreciation.

So, what's the biggest hurdle for people looking to get into fractional real estate investing? In our opinion, it's **lack of awareness and understanding** of the benefits and risks involved. Platforms like Lofty can address these concerns by **providing education and transparency** about the investment process and the potential returns. By doing so, they can help build trust with potential investors and make fractional real estate investing more accessible to a wider audience.

When it comes to managing your real estate investments, one of the biggest decisions you'll face is whether to refinance and pull out equity or hold onto your cash flow. The potential benefits of refinancing are clear: **10-15% return on investment** if you can invest the money wisely. However, this strategy also comes with significant risks, including **taking on more debt** and **potentially reducing your cash flow**. To mitigate these risks, some investors use the **1% rule** as a benchmark, where your monthly rental income should be at least 1% of the property's value to be considered a good investment.

But what about when you're considering refinancing? Is there a similar rule of thumb for determining when to pull out equity versus holding onto cash flow? Unfortunately, there's no one-size-fits-all answer, as the right strategy will depend on your individual financial goals and circumstances. However, **diversification** is key, and that's where fractional real estate marketplaces like Lofty come in. By investing smaller amounts of money, around **$5-10k**, you can gain access to higher-end properties and potentially earn **8-12% annual returns** without breaking the bank.

Despite the potential benefits of fractional investing, there are also **potential downsides** to consider, including **illiquidity** and **limited control** over the investment. To mitigate these risks, it's essential to do your research and choose a reputable platform with a strong track record. You should also **diversify your portfolio** by investing in multiple properties and asset classes to minimize your risk. By taking a thoughtful and informed approach to fractional investing, you can potentially earn strong returns while also **building your credit** and achieving your long-term financial goals.

So what's the bottom line? Whether you're considering refinancing or exploring fractional investing, the key is to **stay informed** and **adapt to changing market conditions**.

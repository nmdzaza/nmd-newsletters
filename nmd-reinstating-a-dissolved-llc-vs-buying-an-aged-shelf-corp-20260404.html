Reinstating A Dissolved Llc Vs Buying An Aged Shelf Corp

April 04, 2026

When it comes to getting funded, entrepreneurs and small business owners often face a dilemma: reinstating a dissolved LLC or buying an aged shelf corp. Both options have their pros and cons, and the right choice depends on your specific situation and goals. **Reinstating a dissolved LLC can be a cost-effective option**, but it's not always the fastest way to get funded. In most states, like Delaware or Florida, reinstatement fees range from $200 to $500, plus any outstanding taxes or penalties.

For example, in California, you're looking at a **$250 reinstatement fee, plus a $20 certification fee, and any back taxes owed**. While the costs may seem relatively low, reinstatement can take several weeks to a few months, which may not be ideal for businesses that need funding quickly. On the other hand, **buying an aged shelf corp can be more expensive upfront**, but it can also get you funded faster. Aged shelf corps typically come with an established credit history, which can be a major advantage when applying for credit.

The cost of an aged shelf corp can range from **$2,000 to $10,000 or more**, depending on the age and credit history of the entity. For instance, a 5-year-old shelf corp with a **700+ credit score** from a vendor like NMD Solutions or Credit Suite can cost around $4,000 to $6,000. While the initial cost may be higher, an aged shelf corp can get you funded in as little as **1-2 weeks**, which can be a game-changer for businesses that need quick access to capital.

When deciding between reinstating a dissolved LLC and buying an aged shelf corp, it's essential to consider your specific situation and goals. **If you're looking for a quick funding solution and have the budget for it, an aged shelf corp might be the way to go**. However, if you're on a tighter budget and have the time to wait, **reinstating a dissolved LLC could be a more cost-effective option**. Ultimately, the right choice depends on your business needs and financial situation.

For example, Home Depot's credit program requires a minimum of **$500 in purchases within 30 days**, but it can help you establish a credit line of up to **$10,000**. This is a crucial step in building your business credit, as it will help you to secure better loan terms and higher credit limits in the future.

Once you have a solid credit profile, you can start looking for government contracts to bid on. Websites like **SAM.gov** or your local government's procurement website can provide a list of available contracts. It's also important to note that some contracts are **set aside for small businesses or minority-owned businesses**, so be sure to take advantage of those opportunities. With the right credit profile and entity setup, you can start bidding on contracts and growing your business.

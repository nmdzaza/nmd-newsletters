Why the Scores Differ
NMD

FICO and
VantageScore
Are Not
The Same Math.

01

Different Weight on Utilization

FICO and VantageScore both count utilization, but VantageScore 4.0 uses "trended data" — meaning it looks at whether your balances are going up or down over time, not just the snapshot. If your utilization is creeping up even slightly, VS 4.0 can be harsher than FICO 8.

VS 4.0 Sensitive to Trends
02

Thin Files Score Differently

FICO 8 requires at least 6 months of history to generate a score. VantageScore 4.0 can score you with just one month of history. This sounds like a win — but it also means newer accounts impact VS scores faster and more dramatically than FICO.

VS Scores More People
03

Collections Treated Differently

FICO 9 ignores paid collections. FICO 8 still counts them. VantageScore 4.0 ignores medical debt in collections but weighs other collections more heavily in some profiles. The same collections account can affect each model completely differently.

Model-Specific Logic
04

Industry-Specific FICO Versions Are Even More Different

Auto lenders frequently use FICO Auto Score 2, 4, or 8 — versions that amplify auto loan payment history. If you've ever had a late payment on a car note, these models punish it harder than standard FICO 8. You can have a 750 FICO 8 and a 699 Auto Score simultaneously.

Know Before You Apply
NMD

What Lenders
Actually Pull
by Loan Type.

🏦

Credit Cards

Most card issuers use FICO Score 8 or 9 — sometimes bureau-specific versions. Amex tends to favor EX. Chase pulls EX heavily for cards. Capital One pulls all three. myFICO is reasonably accurate here.

🚗

Auto Loans

Dealers and direct lenders commonly use FICO Auto Score 2, 4, or 8 — sometimes VantageScore. This is where the 41-point surprise happens most often. Your FICO 8 means almost nothing here without checking Auto-specific scores.

🏡

Mortgages

Historically required Classic FICO (Mortgage Score 2/4/5) via tri-merge. Since July 2025, lenders can now choose VantageScore 4.0 for Fannie/Freddie loans. This is the biggest industry shift in decades. Know both scores before house hunting.

💳

Free Tools (Credit Karma, Experian)

Show VantageScore 3.0 — a model almost no lender uses for actual decisions. It's useful for tracking directional movement, but the number itself is not what lenders see. Never apply based solely on a free score tool.

🏦

Personal Loans

Varies widely. Online lenders (SoFi, LightStream) often use FICO 8. Some use VantageScore for prescreening. Fintech lenders increasingly use VS 4.0 or 5.0 for initial decisions, then may pull FICO for final underwriting.

🏢

Credit Unions

This is the wild card. Credit unions have the most variety. Some use FICO 8. Some use older FICO 4 or 5. Some use VS 4.0. Almost none of them tell you in advance. Always ask which bureau and which model before applying.

Ai Agent Credentials Live In The Same Box As Untrusted Code: Two New Architectures Show Where The Blast Radius Actually

April 11, 2026

As AI continues to permeate various aspects of our lives, it's becoming increasingly important to address the security concerns surrounding these technologies. Recently, two new architectures have been introduced that highlight the need for zero trust when it comes to AI systems. This means that businesses must reevaluate their security protocols to ensure that sensitive information is protected from untrusted code. The concept of zero trust extends to AI, emphasizing the need to shift from access control to action control, as noted by Cisco's Jeetu Patel.

A key concern is that AI agent credentials often live in the same box as untrusted code, creating a significant security risk. This was a common theme in four separate RSAC 2026 keynotes, with Microsoft's Vasu Jakkal emphasizing the importance of extending zero trust to AI. This shift in approach is crucial for businesses to protect themselves from potential security breaches. By prioritizing action control, businesses can ensure that their AI systems are secure and reliable.

In other news, Intuit's TurboTax team has demonstrated the potential of AI to streamline complex regulatory implementations. By using AI to compress a 900-page unstructured document into a functional workflow, the team was able to adapt to new tax code in a remarkably short time frame. This achievement highlights the potential for AI to save time and resources for businesses in regulated industries. Entrepreneurs can learn from Intuit's example, applying similar AI-powered solutions to navigate complex regulatory environments and improve their bottom line.

However, the increasing use of AI also raises concerns about the potential misuse of these technologies. A recent lawsuit against OpenAI alleges that the company ignored warnings about a ChatGPT user who was using the platform to stalk and harass his ex-girlfriend. This lawsuit highlights the need for AI developers to monitor and mitigate the potential misuse of their platforms. Business owners must consider the potential risks and consequences of integrating AI-powered tools into their operations and prioritize user safety and well-being.

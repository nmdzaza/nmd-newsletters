When Disputing Errors On Your Credit Report, Focus On The Metro2 "Account Status

June 06, 2026

When it comes to disputing errors on your credit report, it's essential to focus on the details that matter. One crucial field to examine is the Metro2 "Account Status" field. An incorrect code here can be a clear violation of 15 U.S.C. § 1681e(b) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). This section of the law requires that credit reporting agencies and furnishers ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information they provide. If the Account Status code is incorrect, it can have a significant impact on your credit score and overall financial health.

To effectively dispute errors related to the Account Status field, you need to request that the furnisher provide a complete "Account History" to verify the status code. When making this request, be sure to cite 15 U.S.C. § 1681s-2(a)(1) for FDCPA compliance. This section of the law outlines the responsibilities of furnishers when it comes to investigating and responding to disputes. By referencing this specific section, you can ensure that the furnisher is aware of their obligations and is more likely to take your dispute seriously.

If the furnisher fails to respond or correct the error, it's time to take further action. You can file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), referencing 12 U.S.C. § 5536. This section of the law gives the CFPB the authority to regulate and enforce federal consumer financial laws, including the FCRA and FDCPA. By filing a complaint, you can help ensure that the furnisher is held accountable for their actions and that your credit report is accurate and up-to-date.

The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) is a powerful tool for consumers to dispute and correct errors on their credit reports. One often-overlooked section, **FCRA 609(e)**, requires furnishers to verify debts within 30 days of a dispute. However, most furnishers neglect to provide the required E-Oscar validation, making it a **goldmine for disputes**. By understanding and leveraging this section, you can take control of your credit report and potentially remove inaccurate or unverifiable debts.

To start the dispute process, you'll need to send a **certified letter** to the furnisher, citing **15 USC 1681s-2(b)**. This letter should specifically request that the furnisher provide the **original contract**, **payment history**, and **proof of ownership**. It's essential to be thorough and clear in your request, as this will help to ensure that the furnisher takes your dispute seriously. Simultaneously, you should file a dispute with the credit reporting agency (CRA), referencing the same information and citing the same laws.

If the furnisher fails to respond or provides incomplete information, you may be able to **sue for damages** under **15 USC 1681n**.

Another important consideration for investors is the decision between investing in a **duplex versus two single-family homes**. The trade-offs between these options are significant, with duplexes often requiring more upfront renovation costs but potentially offering **higher rental yields**. For example, a duplex might require $20,000 to $50,000 in renovation costs, but could generate $2,000 to $4,000 in monthly rental income. In contrast, two single-family homes might require less upfront renovation costs, but could generate $1,500 to $3,000 in monthly rental income. Understanding these trade-offs is key to making **informed investment decisions**.

The Lofty marketplace is one platform that's making fractional real estate investing more accessible to individual investors. By allowing investors to **purchase fractions of properties**, Lofty is reducing the barriers to entry for real estate investing. For example, an investor might be able to purchase a 5% stake in a property for $5,000, rather than needing to come up with $100,000 to purchase the property outright. This could potentially open up real estate investing to a wider range of people, including those who might not have been able to afford it otherwise, and provide **greater flexibility** in investment portfolios.

As with any investment, there are **potential risks and benefits** to consider. On the one hand, fractional real estate investing can provide a **lower barrier to entry** and allow investors to diversify their portfolios. On the other hand, it can also come with **higher fees** and **less control** over the investment property. To mitigate these risks, it's essential to **conduct thorough research** and **develop a solid investment strategy**. This includes understanding the local real estate market, assessing the property's potential for appreciation, and evaluating the potential for rental income.

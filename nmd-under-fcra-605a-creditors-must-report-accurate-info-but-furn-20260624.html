Under Fcra 605(A), Creditors Must Report Accurate Info, But Furnisher Obligation

June 24, 2026

When it comes to credit reporting, accuracy is key. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) 605(a), creditors are required to report accurate information. However, the obligations of furnishers under 611(a) are often overlooked. This can lead to errors on your credit report that can negatively impact your credit score. To effectively dispute these errors, it's essential to understand your rights and take a strategic approach.

**Don't just send a generic dispute letter**. Instead, specify the incorrect Metro2 data fields, such as **Date of First Delinquency** or **Current Balance**, and demand a reinvestigation under 611(a)(1). This will ensure that the furnisher takes your dispute seriously and takes the necessary steps to correct the error. Additionally, cite **FDCPA 808(1)** to remind them of their duty to validate debts. This will help to establish that you are aware of your rights and will hold them accountable for their obligations.

When sending your dispute, **use certified mail** to create a paper trail. This will provide proof of mailing and ensure that the furnisher receives your dispute. It's also essential to **keep a record of all correspondence**, including dates, times, and details of conversations. This will help you to track the progress of your dispute and provide evidence if needed.

To take your credit repair efforts to the next level, **check your report for soft inquiries and duplicate tradelines**. These can be disputed under FCRA 609 and can have a significant impact on your credit score. Soft inquiries, which are inquiries that do not affect your credit score, can still be removed if they are unnecessary or inaccurate. Duplicate tradelines, on the other hand, can be removed if they are identical to another tradeline on your report.

By understanding your rights and taking a strategic approach to credit repair, you can **boost your credit score** and achieve your financial goals.

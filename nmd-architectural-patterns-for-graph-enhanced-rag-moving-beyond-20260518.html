However, as AI systems become more prevalent in the enterprise, they are **replacing human experts**, which poses a significant risk. These systems rely on human expertise to learn and improve, but by replacing the very experts they need to learn from, they are creating a **self-reinforcing cycle** where AI systems are depriving themselves of the expertise they need to function effectively. This phenomenon highlights the need for businesses to be aware of this risk and take steps to **mitigate it**, ensuring the long-term effectiveness of their AI systems. By recognizing the importance of human expertise, businesses can take a more balanced approach to AI adoption, combining the benefits of technology with the value of human insight.

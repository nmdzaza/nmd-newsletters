Dealership disputes are becoming increasingly common, with many individuals facing issues related to down payments and credit dings from dealerships. If you're one of them, it's essential to know how to dispute invalid inquiries and collections. The first step is to **check your credit report** for any unauthorized inquiries from dealerships. Look for the Metro2 field "CB", which indicates the credit reporting agency's response to your dispute. If you find an unauthorized inquiry, it's likely that the dealership ran your credit without your permission.

To dispute the inquiry, you'll need to **write a dispute letter** to the credit bureau, citing **FCRA section 615(a)**, which requires prior consent for credit inquiries. In your letter, specify the **date and time** you informed the dealership not to run your credit, and mention the **pre-approval letter** you provided. Clearly state that the dealership's actions were in violation of FCRA section 615(a) and demand that the inquiry be removed. This is a critical step in protecting your credit and ensuring that your report is accurate.

In addition to disputing unauthorized inquiries, you should also **check for collections** related to down payment disputes. If a dealership has sent you to collections, dispute the collection with the credit bureau, citing **FDCPA section 807(8)**, which prohibits false or misleading representations. It's essential to **keep a record** of all communications, including dates, times, and details of conversations. This will help you track your progress and ensure that you're taking the necessary steps to resolve the issue.

To avoid similar issues in the future, **inform dealerships in writing** not to run your credit, and provide a copy of your **pre-approval letter**. This will help prevent unauthorized inquiries and ensure that you're protected. When dealing with a collection agency, ensure you're **responding to the original creditor**, as required by **FDCPA section 809(a)**. By taking these steps, you can protect your credit and avoid costly mistakes.

Disputing invalid inquiries and collections can be a complex and time-consuming process. However, with the right tools and resources, you can take control of your credit and ensure that your report is accurate. **Key points to remember** include checking your credit report regularly, disputing unauthorized inquiries, and keeping a record of all communications. By following these steps, you can protect your credit and achieve your financial goals.

Building business credit is a crucial step in unlocking funding for your small business, but for many entrepreneurs, it's a daunting task. With hundreds of business credit profiles under my belt, I'm here to share the insider knowledge on how to get started. **Establishing a solid entity structure is the first step**, which means filing for an LLC or corporation in a business-friendly state like Delaware or Wyoming. This foundation is essential for creating a separate business credit profile, allowing you to build credit that's independent of your personal credit score.

When it comes to building business credit, **the order of vendor credit applications matters**. You'll want to start with easy approvals like Nav, CreditBuilderPlus, or Fundbox, which can give you an initial credit limit of $1,000 to $5,000. From there, you can work your way up to higher-limit vendors like Home Depot, Lowe's, or Shell, which can offer credit limits of $10,000 to $50,000 or more. The key is to apply for credit in the right order and to make regular payments to build a strong credit history. **Consistency and patience are key** in this process, as building business credit takes time and effort.

One strategy that can give you a head start is using a **shelf corporation**, which is an aged corporation that's already been established and has a credit history. This can save you months or even years of time and effort building credit from scratch. I've seen entrepreneurs use shelf corps to get approved for $100,000 or more in funding within weeks of setup. If you're interested in learning more about shelf corps or building business credit, I'm here to help. **Don't be afraid to ask questions** and seek guidance from those who have experience in building business credit.

As you navigate the process of building business credit, it's essential to stay focused on your goals and **keep a long-term perspective**. Building credit is a marathon, not a sprint, and it requires consistent effort and attention to detail. By following the right steps and using the right strategies, you can unlock the funding you need to take your business to the next level.

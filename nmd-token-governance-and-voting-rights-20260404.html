Token Governance And Voting Rights

April 04, 2026

When it comes to real estate tokenization, **token governance and voting rights** are often misunderstood. As a partial owner of a property through Lofty, you're not just a passive income receiver - you have a say in the decision-making process. This means that you, along with other token holders, get to vote on key aspects of the property's management, such as **renovation plans**, **leasing agreements**, and even the **sale of the property**. This level of control is unprecedented in traditional real estate investing, where individual investors often have limited influence over the property's management.

In terms of numbers, let's consider a scenario where a property is tokenized into 10,000 tokens, each representing **0.01% ownership**. If you hold 100 tokens, you own **0.1% of the property** and have **0.1% of the voting power**. This may seem insignificant, but when combined with the voting power of other like-minded token holders, you can collectively influence the decision-making process. For instance, if **500 token holders**, each with **0.1% voting power**, band together, they can exercise **5% of the total voting power**. This collective power can be a game-changer in shaping the property's future.

The framework for **token governance and voting rights** is typically outlined in the token's **smart contract**. This contract dictates the rules and regulations for voting, including the **percentage of tokens required to pass a proposal**. In some cases, a **simple majority (51%)** may be sufficient, while in others, a **supermajority (67% or 75%)** may be required. Understanding these rules is crucial to effectively exercising your voting rights and having a meaningful impact on the property's management. It's essential to stay informed about the specific rules and regulations governing your tokenized property to make the most of your investment.

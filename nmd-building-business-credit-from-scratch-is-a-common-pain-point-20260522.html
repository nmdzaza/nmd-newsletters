Building business credit from scratch - it's a pain point many new entrepreneurs face. With a surge in LLC filings in California, it's clear that many of you are looking for ways to set up a solid business credit profile. But here's the thing: **most gurus will steer you wrong**. They'll tell you to start with a secured credit card, but that's not the best first step. Instead, you should focus on building a strong foundation with **vendor credit**.

Start by establishing **Net 30 accounts** with vendors like Summa Office Supplies, Grainger, or Quill. These vendors will report your payment history to the business credit bureaus, helping you establish a positive credit profile. This is a crucial step, as it lays the groundwork for your business credit journey. From there, you can move on to **revolving credit** like a Visa or Amex card. But don't get ahead of yourself - **vendor credit is the foundation** upon which you'll build your business credit profile.

Now, some of you might be thinking about using a **shelf corp** to speed up the process. Let me tell you, it's not as simple as just buying an aged entity and expecting instant credit. You need to understand how to properly set up and use a shelf corp to maximize its potential. For example, if you're filing in California, you'll need to make sure your entity is properly registered with the Secretary of State and has an **EIN from the IRS**. This is just the starting point - there are many other factors to consider when using a shelf corp to build business credit.
